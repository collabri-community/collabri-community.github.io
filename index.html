Collabri

Permanent identifier root at https://w3id.org/collabri/.

This site resolves locally-minted terms in Collabri schemas. Terms whose identity already lives in an external ontology (FIBO, ODRL, FRAPO, PROV, PAV, FaBiO, DCAT, DOAP, IAO, ADMS, schema.org, etc.) resolve at their source; Collabri only mints what isn't already there.

Schemas

Statement of Work (SOW) Schema

LinkML schema for contract-scoped SOWs: TaskTeam → Task → recursive Subtasks → typed Deliverables, with PROV/PAV versioning and a ChangeProposal / Approval layer (“GitHub for contracts”). Aligned with FIBO Contracts (primary), ODRL, FRAPO, PROV-O, PAV, and more.

Namespace: https://w3id.org/collabri/sow/

Identifier conventions

  • https://w3id.org/collabri/{schema}/{Term} — locally-minted class, slot, or enum (e.g. collabri/sow/TaskTeam).
  • Each term resolves to its entry in the LinkML-generated schema browser at the same URL.
  • Content negotiation (Turtle, RDF/XML, JSON-LD) will be added when the OWL artifacts are published.

Internal artifacts (controlled-document SOPs, instance data) are out of scope for w3id.org/collabri and live in internal systems.
